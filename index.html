A thousand hands on your desktop.

Senju is a computer-use agent swarm for the Mac. Each agent has its own cursor, working in your team’s real apps while you keep working in yours.

Download for Mac

A swarm you can interrupt.

Most computer-use agents run out of sight and hand back a result. Senju's agents work in front of you, in your own browser and Mac apps, and they stay answerable the whole time.

  • Write to the lead or to any worker while it works. It replans.
  • Pause an agent, take over its window, hand it back.
  • Sign-ins and anything sent in your name wait for your click.

One swarm, several desks.

In a team, one agent can read something on your Mac and write it on a teammate's. While it rests, its cursor is docked on every screen. When someone calls it, it goes to that screen and leaves the others.

  • Nobody's Mac is used until its owner allows it, each session.
  • Everyone can talk to the lead. Every message carries a name.
  • A request at someone's computer is shown to that person only.
  • What the team learns is remembered with who it came from.

Every job leaves a skill behind.

When a job ends, the lead writes a short retrospective and saves what it learned as a skill: a plain Markdown file you can read, edit or delete. The next job that resembles it starts from that file. In a team, skills are shared and every revision is kept.

Install

  1. Download the app and drag it to Applications. Apple silicon or Intel.
  2. Open it the first time from System Settings. This build is not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS stops it once. Go to Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Add your Anthropic API key. The app asks the first time you run a job. Agents run on your own key, which stays on your Mac.
  4. Press Command Shift Space and say what you want done. The same keys hide the bar and bring it back.
